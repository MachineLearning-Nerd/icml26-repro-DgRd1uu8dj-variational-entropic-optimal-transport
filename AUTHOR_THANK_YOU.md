# Thank you to the authors

Thank you to Roman Dyachenko, Nikita Gushchin, Kirill Sokolov, Petr Mokrov, Evgeny Burnaev, and Alexander Korotin for making the VarEOT objective, theorem structure, and executable training procedure available to inspect.

The separation between the exact variational identities, finite-sample and approximation statements, and Algorithm 1 made it possible to build a claim-specific audit instead of treating the paper as one indivisible result. The explicit source/version distinction also made the v1 judged contracts and v2 revisions visible. This repository is intended as a respectful reproducibility record and a contribution to transparent scientific discussion.
